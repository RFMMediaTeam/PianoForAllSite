Questions

Before you press play.

Do I need a real piano?

A basic 61-key keyboard works fine to start. Weighted keys are nicer but not required for the first several books.

I've never played anything. Will I be lost?

No. Book 1 assumes zero experience. You'll play a recognizable song in your first sitting — that's the point of the chord-first approach.

Can I read music by the end?

Yes. Notation is introduced gradually starting around Book 3, once you already understand what the notes are doing. That order matters.

How is this different from YouTube?

YouTube is a scattered library. Pianoforall is a sequenced path. Every lesson assumes what came before and prepares what comes next.

Is the payment really one-time?

Yes. No subscription, no upsells inside. You download it, it's yours, updates included.

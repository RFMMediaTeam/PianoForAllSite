Do I need a piano to start?

A basic keyboard with at least 49 keys is enough to start. As you progress, a full 88-key piano or weighted keyboard is ideal but not required from day one.

How long until I can actually play something?

Most students play recognizable songs within the first few days of Book 1. Within weeks you'll sound like you've been playing for years. Real progress in months, not years.

Is it suitable for absolute beginners?

Absolutely — the course is specifically designed to take complete beginners to intermediate level faster than any other method. No prior musical experience needed.

Everything — PC, Mac, iOS, Android, tablets and phones. Once downloaded, no internet connection required to use the lessons.

Do I have to be young to learn?

Not at all. Students range from children to 80+. Our oldest testimonials come from students in their 70s and 80s having the time of their lives at the piano.

Will I learn to read sheet music?

Yes — but in a smarter way. You learn to play by ear FIRST, then reading sheet music becomes natural (just like learning to speak before you learn to read).
